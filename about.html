About Attirex

A small independent site about the unglamorous half of owning clothes.

Attirex began with a ruined jumper and the discovery that the symbol on its label had meant something quite specific. Nobody is taught to read care labels. Nobody is taught how much detergent is too much. Both are easy, and both save a great deal of money.

What we publish

Two long guides and three free tools, and nothing else. The Laundry Guide covers sorting, washing, drying and machine maintenance. The Wardrobe Guide covers storage, moths and repair. The tools answer the three questions that arrive in our inbox most often.

We write about construction and technique rather than brands, because the brand on the label is the part that matters least to how long a garment lasts.

How we stay independent

We take no payment, samples or sponsorship in exchange for naming a product, and there are no affiliate or shopping links anywhere on this site. If advertising appears, it is served by a third party and has no influence on what we publish. Nobody can pay to be mentioned here, and nobody can pay to be left out.

How we work

Our guidance comes from published textile and care standards, manufacturers’ own instructions and a lot of ordinary washing. Where the right answer depends on the fibre, the finish or your own machine, we say so instead of inventing a rule. Our Editorial Policy sets out how we research, review and correct what we publish.

Where we stop

We are not a cleaner, a repairer or a brand. For anything valuable, unusual or already damaged, a professional who can hold the garment will always give better advice than a website. We would rather send you to one than guess.

Tell us when we are wrong

If a method fails in your hands or a tool gives an odd answer, write to hello@attirex.com or use the contact page. Corrections are dealt with before anything else, and you will get a reply from a person.
